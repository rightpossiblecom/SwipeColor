# Privacy Policy for SwipeColor

Last updated: February 2026

## Introduction

SwipeColor ("we", "our", or "the app") is a puzzle game available on the Google Play Store. This policy describes what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data We Collect

- **Local progress**: Level progress and unlocked levels are stored only on your device (using local storage). We do not upload this data to any server.
- **No account required**: You can play without creating an account or providing an email or name.
- **No analytics by default**: The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the initial release.

## Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

- Name, email, or other contact information
- Precise or approximate location
- Contacts, photos, or files from your device
- Any data from children under 13

## Permissions

- **Internet**: Used only if you open links (for example, this privacy policy or support). Gameplay does not require internet.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses:

- **Google Play Services**: For app distribution and updates only. Subject to Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

## Data Storage and Security

Progress data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app will remove this data. We do not store personal data on our servers.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
